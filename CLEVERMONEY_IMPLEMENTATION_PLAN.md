## Phase 3: Monetization Strategy

### Revenue Streams (In Order of Implementation)

#### 1. Display Ads (Months 1-6)
**Start With**: Google AdSense
- Easy approval for new sites
- $50-200/month once you hit 10-20K monthly pageviews
- No minimum traffic requirement

**Upgrade To** (at 50K monthly pageviews): Mediavine or AdThrive
- $500-2000/month potential at 50K views
- Better rates, better user experience
- Minimum traffic requirements: Mediavine (50K), AdThrive (100K)

**Implementation**:
- Place ads in header, sidebar, between content sections, end of post
- Don't overdo it—3-4 ad placements per page maximum

#### 2. Affiliate Marketing (Start Immediately)
**High-converting financial affiliates**:

**Banking & Credit Cards**:
- Credit Karma - Free credit scores
- NerdWallet - Credit card comparisons
- CIT Bank, Marcus by Goldman Sachs - High-yield savings accounts

**Investing Platforms**:
- M1 Finance - Free investing platform
- Betterment - Robo-advisor
- Webull - Trading platform with signup bonuses

**Financial Tools**:
- Personal Capital - Free financial dashboard
- YNAB (You Need A Budget) - Budgeting software
- Mint - Free budgeting app

**Education**:
- Udemy - Financial courses
- Books via Amazon Associates

**Expected Revenue**: $200-1000/month at 20K monthly visitors with 2-3% conversion

#### 3. Digital Products (Months 6-12)
Create and sell:
- **Budget Templates** ($9-19): Excel/Sheets templates for various budgeting methods
- **Debt Payoff Calculators** ($9): Interactive spreadsheets
- **Investment Planning Workbooks** ($29): Step-by-step investing guides
- **Financial Independence Calculators** ($19): FIRE planning tools

**Expected Revenue**: $300-1500/month once established

#### 4. Email Newsletter + Sponsored Content (Months 12+)
- Build email list from day 1 (use ConvertKit or MailChimp)
- Weekly newsletter with money tips
- Sponsored newsletter spots: $100-500 each at 5000+ subscribers
- Sponsored blog posts: $200-1000 each at 50K+ monthly views
